## Suggested settings for this data

### 1. Apply the vol_return clip — the one remaining open item

**vol_return** max is **still 1836.1×** (mean=1.87, std=28.0). The clip recommended in the prior eval was not applied for this training run.

**Why this still matters**: RobustScaler's IQR parameters are unaffected by the outlier, but after scaling, any window containing this bar has a `vol_return` value hundreds of standard deviations above the median. The autoencoder encountered this during all 500 epochs and had to "waste" capacity trying to reconstruct it. The loss improvements we're seeing (0.58 → 0.47) likely reflect better LR scheduling, not improved representation of normal market behaviour. The outlier bar is distorting reconstruction loss upward.

**Action**: Add to `add_features()` in `src/v2/backend/services/storage.py`:
```python
df['vol_return'] = df['vol_return'].clip(upper=df['vol_return'].quantile(0.99))
df['trade_count_ratio'] = df['trade_count_ratio'].clip(upper=df['trade_count_ratio'].quantile(0.99))
```
This is a one-time code change. All future training runs will use the clipped values automatically.

### 2. Consider gamma=0.995 to stay productive for ~460 epochs

**Current → Suggested**: `gamma` 0.99 → **0.995** (optional, next retrain)

**Why**: With gamma=0.99 from 1e-4 over 500 epochs, the LR crosses below 1e-5 at epoch ~229:

```
epoch 229:  1e-4 × 0.99^229 ≈ 1e-5    ← productive learning ends here
epoch 500:  1e-4 × 0.99^500 = 6.57e-7  ← matches stored final_lr
```

The model ran epochs 229–500 with LR in the marginal-to-dead range. The tiny improvement at the final epoch (final_val_loss 7e-6 better than best_val_loss) suggests the model was still learning at the limit of its LR capacity.

With **gamma=0.995**:
```
epoch 459:  1e-4 × 0.995^459 ≈ 1e-5    ← productive learning ends here  
epoch 500:  1e-4 × 0.995^500 = 8.16e-6  ← close to, but above, 1e-5
```

This keeps the optimizer meaningful for ~460 of 500 epochs. The expected improvement over gamma=0.99 is modest (marginal territory 229–459 vs dead territory 229–459), but the vol_return clip (above) is likely to have a larger impact. Do the clip first, assess the result, then consider the gamma tweak.
